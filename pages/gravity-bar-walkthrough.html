🪐 Gravity from a Rod: Local Mass Approximation

Approximate gravitational force by sampling short rod segments and tracking how distance changes force.

🎯 Introduction

When mass is spread along a rod, each tiny piece contributes to gravitational pull on a nearby point mass. A useful way to build intuition is to split the rod into equal segments and approximate one segment's contribution at a time.

Key Concept: Local Force Contribution

If a segment has mass \(\Delta m\), the point mass is \(m_p\), and distance is \(D\), then a local contribution has the form:

\(\Delta F \propto \frac{G\,\Delta m\,m_p}{D^2}\)

As segment count \(N\) increases, each segment gets smaller. This is the stepping stone to integration.
